In a world increasingly full of AI-generated media, how do you know what's real?

C2PA Verify reads the Content Credentials (C2PA) embedded in a photo, video or PDF and tells you, at a glance, where it came from: who signed it, the tool that created it, whether it has been edited or generated by AI, and whether the signer is one you can trust.

Pick a file from your device, or share one to C2PA Verify from any other app, and get a clear verdict:

• Signed & trusted — the file is signed by an authority you trust and hasn't been tampered with
• Signed by an unknown party — valid credentials, but from a signer you haven't trusted
• Invalid / tampered — the credentials don't match the content
• No Content Credentials — the file carries no C2PA data at all

AI-generated and AI-modified content is clearly flagged, as are camera captures, software-created content, and ordinary edits.

Works with still images (JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, HEIC, AVIF, TIFF, raw and more), MP4 video, and PDF.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• See the signer, the claim generator (the tool that made the file), and when it was signed
• Read a plain-language timeline of everything that happened to it — created, opened, cropped, colour-adjusted, AI-edited, and more
• Dig into the full manifest: signature and certificate details, assertions, ingredients, and validation results, right down to the raw JSON
• Manage which certificate authorities you trust, with your own allow/deny rules
• Export a shareable verification report with the verdict laid over it

PRIVACY

C2PA Verify reads your files entirely on your device. It does not upload them anywhere.

ABOUT C2PA

C2PA (the Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity) is an open standard for content provenance — the same Content Credentials supported by a growing number of cameras and content-editing tools. C2PA Verify is the "last mile": surfacing that information to you in a way that's actually readable.

Free and open source. MIT licensed.
